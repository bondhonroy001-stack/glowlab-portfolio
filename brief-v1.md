# GlowLab: Design Brief v1

We're building GlowLab, a clean/natural color cosmetics brand. Makeup, not skincare: lipsticks, blushes, eyeshadow palettes to start. The idea is to be the indie alternative for people who don't want to pay big-brand prices or put parabens/sulfates on their face, but still want something that looks good on camera and works with a range of skin tones.

We want a full storefront: browse by category, see a product with real shade swatches (not just a hex chip), add to bag, checkout with card or PayPal. Two kinds of buyers matter here: younger shoppers who find us through TikTok/Instagram and buy on trend and price, and slightly older shoppers who actually read the ingredient list before anything else. Both mobile and desktop need to work well, since people browse on their phones but often check out on desktop.

We don't have a logo or brand palette yet; that's part of what needs to get built alongside the site. No real payment gateway integration for now, this is a portfolio piece, not a live store. Would like to launch a first version soon so it can go into a portfolio.
