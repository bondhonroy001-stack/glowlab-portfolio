# Context: GlowLab

**What this file is for:** who GlowLab's users actually are. It overrides the defaults under `Who I am` in `principles/claude-contract.md`, wherever the two disagree.

**Why Claude needs it:** it reads this on every task in this folder. Whatever is vague here comes back as guesswork.

## Your context block

GlowLab is clean/natural color cosmetics (makeup, not skincare), and it pulls two different buyers, not one. The first is 18-25, price-conscious, finds shades through TikTok/Instagram trends and won't buy without checking reviews or UGC video first. The second is 26-35, skeptical of big-brand parabens and sulfates, reads the ingredient list before the price tag.

Audience is global, mixed, heaviest weighted toward the US, same as Nova. This overrides the root default of a Bangladesh-based audience; shipping estimates, return windows and currency need to read as "depends on where you are," not hardcoded to one country.

Both buy for themselves, no gifting, no approval chain, but the younger group still confirms with a friend's opinion or a review before checking out.

Surface is genuinely both, not mobile-only: browsing and discovery happen on phone (scrolling in bed, at night), but purchase often happens on desktop, at work, during a break, because typing card details on a small screen feels riskier to them.

They've used Sephora/Nykaa-style platforms before, so they expect shade-matching visuals and swatches; without a "how this looks on skin" preview, they don't trust a shade enough to buy it sight-unseen.

Payment: standard card checkout (Visa/Mastercard, plus PayPal for the 26-35 group, who trust it more than entering card details on a new indie site). One flow, one screen, no redirect-and-return gateway.

What they don't trust: product photos that look more filtered than real skin. Without a real-skin swatch or an honest before/after, a product photo reads as a marketing image, not proof, and they leave.
