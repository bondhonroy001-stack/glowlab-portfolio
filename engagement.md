# Engagement: <client name>

**What this file is for:** the agreement about the work, as opposed to the work itself. Scope, what is out, revisions, who signs off.

**Why it matters more than it looks:** this is the file that decides whether the job is profitable. A brief tells you what to design. This tells you when you have finished, and what happens when they ask for more.

**If you work on a product rather than for a client**, this is still your file. Replace "client" with "stakeholders" and it does the same job: who decides, who can veto, what is in this cycle and what is not.
